# 07 — Monetization

Pricing for the **Indian market**, where willingness-to-pay is real but price-sensitive, and where USD pricing kills conversion.

---

## Pricing principles

1. **₹ pricing, not USD.** $20/mo lands as ₹1,700/mo and looks expensive in India. Price natively.
2. **Monthly + annual + one-time hire-bonus** (offer one-time, contingent on getting hired).
3. **Free tier that is genuinely useful**, not a 3-day trial. Users need to feel the product helps before paying.
4. **Open-source core**, hosted convenience layer is paid. (Like Career-Ops, like Plausible, like Cal.com.)
5. **No hidden API costs** — user brings their own LLM keys, pays Anthropic/OpenAI/Gemini directly. We make money on convenience, not arbitrage.

---

## Tiers

### Free — "Self-hosted core"

- Download the desktop app
- Bring your own LLM API keys
- All scoring, all tailoring, all referral discovery
- Manual submission only (Tier 3)
- 5 applies/day cap
- Local-only, no cloud sync

**Why free?** Open-source community + virality + DPDP-friendly + the OG Career-Ops did exactly this.

---

### Pro — ₹499 / month  *(or ₹4,999/year)*

- Everything in Free, plus:
- Cloud sync across devices
- Tier 2 + Tier 1 submission flows
- 25 applies/day
- Ghost-Job Radar + Salary Leak + Bond detector
- Auto-follow-ups
- Stealth Mode
- Browser Co-Pilot extension
- WhatsApp recruiter assist (drafts, doesn't send)
- Email support

**Anchor:** comparable products charge ₹999–₹2,000/mo. We undercut with more value.

---

### Plus — ₹1,499 / month  *(or ₹14,999/year)*

- Everything in Pro, plus:
- Skill-Claim Prover with full evidence graph
- Interview Time Machine
- Negotiation Co-Pilot
- Application DNA learning
- 50 applies/day
- WhatsApp recruiter full automation (Business API)
- Voice interface (Hindi + English)
- Priority support

**Target persona:** Priya (4-yr SDE), Karan (senior PM). They'll pay because one good offer is worth crores over a career.

---

### Cohort — ₹999 per person / month, min 3 people

For friend groups (Tier-2/3 fresher cohorts especially).
- All Pro features
- Cohort Mode (shared listings, peer CV review, group accountability)
- 30% cheaper per seat than individual Pro

---

### Hire Bonus — ₹4,999 one-time (contingent)

For users who don't want subscription:
- Pay only when you accept an offer
- Free until then
- Payable after first salary credits
- Honor system + offer letter verification

**Why this works:** removes signup friction, aligns incentives, generates word-of-mouth ("It's literally free unless you get hired").

---

### Enterprise / Campus — ₹X per student per year, custom

- Sell to Tier-2/3 college placement cells
- Bulk seats for graduating batch
- Branded portal for the college
- Reporting on placement outcomes

**Pricing range:** ₹500–1,500 per student/yr depending on volume and college tier. A 600-student batch at ₹800 = ₹4.8L/year per college. Repeatable across hundreds of colleges.

---

## What we do NOT charge for

- Account creation
- Resume parsing
- Reading job listings
- Browsing the open-source repo
- Interview prep on the open-source tier (it's a moat for trust)

These numbers assume aggressive growth post-PMF. Phase 1 is sub-zero (research + build). Phase 2 onset is the first real revenue moment.

## Pricing experiments to run

1. **Hire Bonus vs subscription** — which converts better in early signups?
2. **₹499 vs ₹699 Pro tier** — Indian users react sharply to round-number psychology
3. **Annual discount %** — 17% (2 months free) vs 30% (4 months free) — measure LTV impact
4. **Cohort minimum size** — 3 vs 5 — affects virality vs ARPU

---

## Things to avoid

- ❌ Surge pricing during placement season (looks predatory)
- ❌ "Premium support" upsell (support is brand-defining; don't gate it)
- ❌ "Pay to be featured to recruiters" until reverse-marketplace is real
- ❌ USD-default pricing pages
